Research Interests

My research focuses on low-level vision and image restoration, with an emphasis on recovering fine details while preserving image structure. I am particularly interested in spatial-frequency representations for ultra-high-definition image restoration and efficient super-resolution.

I also work on multimodal generative AI (AIGC), spanning data engineering, image and video generation and editing, and deployment in real-world products. I am interested in connecting high-quality data pipelines with controllable, efficient generative models, and translating research advances into practical visual creation tools.
